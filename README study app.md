# Whelps StudyApp

**Tell it your subjects and free hours. It builds your study week.**

Whelps StudyApp is a study planner for students. It arranges a full study timetable automatically, reminds you when your next session starts, keeps your notes in one place, and shows your consistency with a study grid you can share.

By **NMK Technologies company**.

> **Status:** in planning. Features below describe what we are building, not what is finished yet.

---

## Who it is for

- **First audience:** university students
- **Later:** secondary school students (exam modes, subject presets, parent view)
- **Languages:** English and Kiswahili at launch, with more (such as German and French) planned

## Features

### Core (first version)
- **Accounts:** a personal space for every student
- **Smart timetable generator:** enter your subjects, lectures, free hours, exam dates and how hard each subject feels. The app fills your gaps with study sessions, gives harder subjects more time, and adds breaks
- **Exam countdown:** days left on the home screen, with study time increasing as exams get closer
- **Reminders:** a notification before each session and a clear view of your next one
- **Notes:** attached to a subject or session
- **Cute templates:** several themes to choose from
- **Study grid and streaks:** a GitHub-style grid with one square per day studied
- **Share cards:** export your timetable or study grid as an image for WhatsApp or Instagram

### Coming after
- Wellness nudges during sessions (water, stretch, rest)
- AI quick questions inside a session or note
- Photo notes
- Sharing with friends (friends-only by default)
- Milestone badges, Pomodoro timer, weekly recap

### Premium
- AI beyond the free daily limit
- Streak revival after the free revives
- Extra templates and themes

## Design principles

- Setup in under 2 minutes
- Cute but clean
- Works well with limited data
- Privacy first: friends-only sharing, no public feed
- Built to support many languages from day one
